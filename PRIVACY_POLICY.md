# Rastro Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Rastro ("the app") is a personal location diary built by [YOUR NAME].

## What the app records
With your permission, Rastro uses your device's location services to record places you visit and an approximate path of your movement. It also uses Apple's geocoding service to turn coordinates into place, city and country names.

## Where your data is stored
All location history is stored **only on your device**. Rastro has no servers and no accounts. We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit your location data to us or any third party. The only outside request is Apple's reverse-geocoding service, which receives coordinates to return place names, subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your control
- Pause tracking anytime in Rastro → Settings.
- Delete all your data in Rastro → Settings → "Delete all my data", or by deleting the app.
- Change or revoke location permission in iOS Settings → Rastro → Location.

## Children
Rastro is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes (for example, if optional cloud sync is added), we'll update this page and the date above.
